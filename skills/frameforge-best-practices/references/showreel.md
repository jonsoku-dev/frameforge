# Motion-graphics review

Judge a showreel, ident, launch or promo against its own brief, audience and intended pace. Rendering complexity or the amount of motion is not a quality measure. A deliberate hold or cut can carry an idea; an effect, camera move or added layer should contribute to the message.

In `prompt.md`, record the concept and visual world, palette, principal states, changes of focus, transitions and settled reading intervals. For factual information, record its source and check that visible states remain consistent.

On a contact sheet, inspect every major state and the final frame. Ask what information each state adds, which element leads the eye, whether the next state follows intelligibly, and whether text is clear at the delivery size. Canvas and shader text is outside the DOM audit. Reconstruct weak scenes where composition, action or timing does not serve their purpose, then inspect the revision.

Review the full film at playback speed and listen to the mix when available. A sheet alone cannot establish pacing, smoothness, reading comfort or audible synchronization. Keep the actual frame-check, flash-check and seam-check results; assess aesthetic thresholds against the brief and report intentional holds or unresolved conflicts without calling a failed or skipped check a pass. Record what was not observed.

# Motion-graphics review

Judge a showreel, ident, launch or promo against its own brief, audience and intended pace. Rendering complexity or the amount of motion is not a quality measure. A deliberate hold or cut can carry an idea; an effect, camera move or added layer should contribute to the message.

In `prompt.md`, record the concept and visual world, palette, principal states, changes of focus, transitions and settled reading intervals. For factual information, record its source and check that visible states remain consistent.

Within the authorized scope, inspect relevant states and the last requested frame. Before rendering a contact sheet or still, check whether asset generation, network requests or other out-of-range effects are permitted; frame selection alone does not prevent them. If this route is not allowed or cannot be assessed, use an authorized observation method or mark the affected observations NOT VERIFIED. Ask what information each observed state adds, which element leads the eye, whether the next state follows intelligibly, and whether text is clear at the delivery size. Canvas and shader text is outside the DOM audit. Reconstruct weak scenes where composition, action or timing does not serve their purpose, then inspect the revision using an authorized method.

Review the authorized output at playback speed and listen to the mix when available. A partial review does not establish whole-film status. A sheet alone cannot establish pacing, smoothness, reading comfort or audible synchronization. Keep the actual frame-check, flash-check and seam-check results; assess aesthetic thresholds against the brief and report intentional holds or unresolved conflicts without calling a failed or skipped check a pass. Record what was not observed.

# Assets

Quality bar: a readable and distinctive silhouette, appropriate pose/expression, coherent material/light and deliberate relationships with type and the scene. Inspect the actual picture at delivery size. Shape counts, uniform outlines or flat fills can describe a particular icon set; they are not universal asset requirements. Licensed raster, authored vector, local 3D and procedural media are all options. For motion films follow `../../frameforge-motion/references/visual-development.md`.

Available routes, selected for the brief and actual authorized tools:
1. **Kit**: `import {Cup, Phone, Pin} from '@/assets'`. Every asset takes `{size, ink, accent, soft, paper, stroke?, radius?, draw?, parts?, style?}`. `draw` 0..1 draws the strokes in; `parts` reveals assembly groups in order. The catalogue with tags is `src/assets/catalog.ts`; the sheet is rendered from `src/assets/Sheet.tsx`.
2. **Resolver**: `examples/samples/<slug>/assets.json` = `{"slug", "palette": {"ink","accent","soft","paper"}, "assets": [{"name", "intent", "source"?: "auto|host|image-command|library|kit", "recolor"?: true}]}` → `npm run art -- <that file>` writes `public/assets/generated/<slug>/<name>.svg`, a manifest and `CREDITS.md`. Providers: a file you placed yourself (`host`), your own image skill through `FRAMEFORGE_IMAGE_COMMAND` (PNG → vectorized), Openverse CC0 SVG, then the kit. Play with `<Art src="/assets/generated/<slug>/<name>.svg" size draw ink accent soft paper />`.
3. **Own drawing**: put reusable artwork in the film's `assets.tsx`; inspect its silhouette, expressive pose and scene integration before extending the animation. Use as much or as little detail as the intended picture requires.

Process-film props should make their identity and action clear. Illustration, suitable icons, licensed media and 3D can serve that goal; inspect the actual composition. A bottle tilting and pouring from its own neck is one example. Kit APIs for it: `<Bottle size label="GIN" tilt={deg}>`, `bottleNeck({size, tilt, x, y})` as the `<Stream from>` point, and `<Glass size fill={0..1} liquid={colour} ice={0..3}>` with `glassLevel(fill)` for the surface height of an oz label.

Real screens (explainers): use supplied or authorized captures (the repo ships none). Put them unmodified in a folder under `public/` and add that folder to `.gitignore`; record DOM-measured rectangles in `prompt.md`. Icons such as `lucide-react` markers require appropriate rights and a visual fit with the composition.

For 3D, choose authored or appropriately licensed models and suitable tools (`frameforge-3d`, or the ThreeCanvas contract in `bespoke.md`). Builtin Blender subjects and Poly Haven models are options, not the required visual identity. Shader presets such as `planet`, `sun`, `sliced` and `orbits` can serve a subject or background when appropriate; their presence does not establish quality. Uniforms are in `docs/reference/generative.md`.

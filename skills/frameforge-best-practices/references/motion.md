# Motion

- Default doctrine: spectacle and continuity. Every second shows at least one active effect or transformation, the camera never fully stops, scenes morph into each other, the frame is layered (foreground type, an effect, a textured background), a new technique on every beat. A calm film is the exception the request names. The bar: `showreel.md`; the rendering helpers: `bespoke.md`.
- Frame function: `const frame = useCurrentFrame()` once at the root; write your own timing helpers as pure functions of the frame (an eased 0..1 span from `interpolate` with `Easing`, a `spring`, a decaying kick from `beats.pulse`).
- Grammar: continuous morph (one hero, never cut; size, radius and colour change, content swaps with a short blur; the camera zooms so each state fills the frame) or joined scenes (every cut a seam). One object carries continuity either way.
- Retargeted values: `springTrack({frame, fps, from, keys: [{at, to}], config})` sums one spring per change, so a value that moves on every beat stays a pure function of the frame. Stretchy indicators: leading and trailing edges on different springs.
- 60 fps films: `<CameraMotionBlur shutterAngle={180} samples={4}>` around the picture; `MotionProbe`, `Audio` and `MotionSound` outside it (frozen copies report nothing).
- Glyph rise masks: use `blockMask` and `MASK_DROP` from `@/blocks` (0.1em above, 0.4em below, 0.3em beside each glyph, layout height unchanged), spaces as `'\u00A0'`. A clip fails the render with the block's name and the fix.
- Text never shows a garbled intermediate state: during a reveal every glyph on screen is whole and at full opacity, or absent. Use `GlyphRise` (word by word, rise 8, stagger 1), `Typewriter` (whole words per step) or `ZoomSwap`; no scramble, no half-erased swaps, no long per-glyph staggers (`by="glyph"` only for a single short word).
- Sizes (1080 short side): headline ≥ 10 %, labels ≥ 3.5 %, icons ≥ 6 %, hero ≥ 35 %; margins 5 %.
- The first second is a striking, frame-filling image or action (a macro crop of the hero, a full-bleed texture, a move already under way); never a small mark or dot on an empty field.
- No near-empty stretch over 10 frames; the camera reframes as often as the states change, in one dominant direction.
- Secondary motion in every shot, so no 0.5 s window is frozen: a camera float (`style={{transform: drift(frame, {x: 10, y: 6, scale: 0.015, period: 240})}}` on the camera layer), parallax between layers (a background layer drifting at half the foreground's amplitude), a breathing element (`scale(${breathe(frame, 60, 0.03)})`) or a ticking counter or progress line. 1 px/s on a headline frame already registers in frame-check; 5–15 px of amplitude over an 8 s period reads as alive without reading as wobble. The end is a live lockup: the name settles within 1 s, then a glow pulse (`<Glow intensity={0.6 + 0.3 * Math.sin(frame / 12)}>`) or drift keeps going. frame-check fails a frozen run over 1 s.
- A process (recipe, how it works, assembly, data flowing) shows each cause and its effect at the same time: the bottle tilts, the stream pours (`<Stream from={lip} to={surface} progress={p} width={14} color={ink} wobble={3} frame={frame} />`, `[tail, head]` progress lets it stop and fall), and the level rises past labelled marks in the same frames. From the pour's first frame the level and its count both move while the stream is visible; a quantity never reads zero under a visible stream. Keep the subject whole in frame and mark it `data-subject` (the audit flags it cropped or covering text); put quantity labels on the object, inside the `data-subject` element.
- A process film's layout evolves step by step: the camera reframes on each step, a tracker fills, completed items accumulate in a rail, the step card moves to make room. Never the same layout repeated with new words.
- Transitions: `TransitionSeries` from `@/packages/transitions` (fade, slide, wipe, flip, clockWipe, iris; linearTiming/springTiming).
- Shapes, paths, noise, blur: `makeStar/makePolygon/...`, `getPointAtLength/evolvePath/interpolatePath`, `noise2D/3D`, `<Trail>`/`<CameraMotionBlur>` all import from `frameforge`.
- Media: `<Video src trimBefore volume playbackRate>`, `<AnimatedImage>`, `<Lottie src>`, `<Img>`; captions via `parseSrt` + `createTikTokStyleCaptions`.
- Existing animation code only: `<Seekable animate={el => el.animate(keyframes, {duration, fill: 'both'})}>` or `useSeekable(ref, factory)` seeks it per frame (`docs/reference/seekable.md`); new motion stays a frame function.
- Full guide: `docs/guides/motion-graphics.md`; explainers: `docs/guides/product-explainer.md`.

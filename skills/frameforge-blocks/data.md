# Data, code and diagram blocks

Reference for the chart, code and diagram blocks in `src/blocks/{charts,code,diagrams}.tsx`. Import them with `import {BarChart} from '@/blocks'`. They share the block contract described in `SKILL.md` (`frame`, `at`, `out`, `duration`, `fps`, `palette`, `fonts`, `size`, `stroke`, `radius`, `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `id`); the Korean reference with every prop is `docs/reference/blocks-data.md`, and `examples/samples/data-reel/Spot.tsx` uses all of them.

Rules these blocks already keep, so you do not have to: hidden before `at` (they return `null`), every motion from `interpolate`/`spring`/`Easing`, lines drawn with `evolvePath` in one `stroke` (default `size × 1.5/3.5`) and one `radius` (default `size × 0.4`), numbers in tabular digits, labels at `size` (keep `size` at or above 3.5% of the short side, 38 px at 1080), text outside any clipping box, `MotionProbe` reports with family `shape` for bars and paths and `card` for panels and rows. Charts carry an 예시 pill by default (`example={false}` turns it off); real figures are forbidden, so keep it on in Korean films.

| Block | Own props (beyond the contract) | Motion | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BarChart` | `data: {label, value}[]`, `max?`, `target?`, `unit?`, `duration` 60 | Bars grow from the baseline with a stagger, counters count up, the target line draws across after the bars landed and keeps a label column on the right | Compare a handful of categories or months; the counters make the values readable at speed |
| `LineChart` | `data: {label, value}[]`, `max?`, `min?` 0, `area?` true, `unit?`, `duration` 60 | Axis draws first, the line draws by length with a leading dot, the area fades in, the last value counts up above the dot | Show a trend over time; the drawing line carries the eye to the last value |
| `BarChartRace` | `snapshots: {label, values: Record<string, number>}[]`, `top?`, `unit?`, `duration` 90 | Rank and width interpolate between neighbouring snapshots, labels ride along, the snapshot label swaps at the midpoint | Rankings changing between snapshots (months, rounds, versions) |
| `Donut` | `value`, `max?` 100, `unit?` '%', `label?`, `thickness?` `size × 0.8`, `duration` 45 | `makePie` ring sweeps to the value, the centre number counts up, the label follows | One share or completion rate that deserves the whole beat |
| `Heatmap` | `rows: number[][]`, `rowLabels?`, `colLabels?`, `order?` 'rows' or 'seeded', `seed?`, `max?`, `duration` 60 | Cells pop in row-major or seeded order (`random(seed-k)`), alpha follows value ÷ max | A two-axis density where the pattern matters more than the numbers |
| `Sparkline` | `data: number[]`, `dot?` true, default box `size × 8` by `size × 2`, `duration` 30 | The line draws in, a dot rides the end; no axes, no labels | A small trend inside a card or next to a number |
| `CodeBlock` | `code`, `lang?` ts/js/python/json/bash/plain, `mode?` typing/highlight/diff/scroll, `highlight?: [from, to]`, `charsPerFrame?` 2, `scrollTo?`, `lines?`, `lineNumbers?` true, `fonts.mono?` | Ink panel rises; typing reveals characters with a caret, highlight sweeps an accent bar over the lines and dims the rest to 0.3, diff slides +/- lines in on green/red tints mixed from `ink`, scroll moves a `lines`-tall window (marked `data-clip="allow"`) to `scrollTo` | Show source: type it in, spotlight lines, show a diff, or scroll to a line. Height ≥ `lines × 1.5 × size + 1.6 × size`; lines are not wrapped |
| `Flowchart` | `nodes: {id, label, shape?: 'rect' or 'pill'}[]`, `edges?: [from, to][]` (default a chain), `direction?` 'row' or 'column' | Nodes pop with a stagger; each connector and its arrowhead draw as one path after both ends landed | A pipeline or decision path of two to six named steps |
| `ProcessSteps` | `steps: string[]`, `active?` (0-based), `duration` 60 | The fill runs along the track to the active step, the nearest step scales up 1.3×; without `active` the beat walks through every step | Onboarding or how-it-works in numbered steps |
| `Timeline` | `items: {date, label}[]`, `duration` 60 | A marker travels the rule, milestones pop as it passes, labels alternate above and below | Dated milestones on one horizontal rule |
| `Compare` | `columns: [string, string]`, `rows: {label, a, b}[]` with `boolean` or `string` cells | Rows rise in sync (6-frame stagger), checks (accent) and crosses (ink) draw once the row landed, strings print | Two plans or options against the same rows |

Catalogue: `CHART_BLOCKS`, `CODE_BLOCKS`, `DIAGRAM_BLOCKS` (`BlockEntry[]` with `id`, `title`, `family`, `props`, `when`, `sample: 'data-reel'`) feed `CATALOG`. The tokeniser is exported as `tokenize(code, lang)` for tests and custom renderings.

Placement pattern from the sample: one paper stage card per film on a soft field (blocks at `x: 160, y: 240, width: 1600, height: 760` at 1920×1080, inside the 5% safe area; the card's margins keep a headline-sized band in every frame for `frame-check`), a 108 px headline above it, each block `at` scene start + 4 and `out` at the next scene start, so the block fades over its last 8 frames and the next headline rises in place.

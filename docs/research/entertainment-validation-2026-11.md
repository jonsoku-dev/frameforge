## User quality rejection and redesign

The user rejected all three entertainment outputs below as poor quality. They remain rejected experiments; legibility fixes and technical passes did not establish sufficient visual craft. The subsequent work revisits assets, shot scale, composition, typography and purposeful action rather than treating the rejection as a request for smaller cosmetic corrections.

It asks the maker to build and inspect a representative shot before extending the timeline. The old shape-count, palette-count, medium and mandatory-motion prescriptions are no longer universal creative rules. This change addresses a documented conflict in the guidance; it does not prove that those rules alone caused the rejected results or that changing prompts automatically improves future films.

Three separate creator agents were requested with `gpt-6-astra` and high reasoning effort for the redesign. This records the accepted tool configuration, not an independently exposed runtime model ID. ## Renderer cancellation found during this work

A prior whole-suite run recorded 310 passes and three failures: two Chrome DevTools connection closures and one `fetch failed` response. Concurrent film rendering and later host disconnection were observed, but neither resource contention nor the cancellation defect below is established as the cause of those three failures.

A separate, concrete defect was reproduced: the previous snapshot CLI's SIGTERM handler exited before its page cleanup, so signalling only the renderer could leave its real Chrome child alive. An isolated baseline failed the new direct-signal test after waiting five seconds for Chrome to exit. The revised renderer tracks its launched browsers and attempts to terminate them before exiting; the direct-signal and ordinary MCP cancellation cases both passed. The ordinary MCP case also passed on the baseline, so it is not claimed as a deterministic reproduction of the leak. They do not establish complete cleanup of every Chrome helper, separate session or ordinary render temporary directory.

## Limits

These are local experiments, not a benchmark across models or proof of professional-quality equivalence. Neither production was inspected through actual normal-speed playback or direct listening; the stills, mobile-size downsampling and waveform measurements do not establish perceived rhythm, phone-screen legibility, audio quality or sync. The films were produced through collaboration agents, not a verified fresh Codex app session. Model identity for the game and music production was not exposed to those agents. Existing global Frameforge skills pointed to a separate checkout, so this work does not prove that the latest plugin is installed or automatically loaded in a new Codex session. Artifacts remain local and are identified by hashes above.

An independently prompted collaboration agent also created the same factual football brief without seeing prior output. This shows that exposing the workflow is not proof that an agent will produce reference-level continuity. It was not an independent Codex app session.
